# Headphones

Original model and textures by nisu, published 2020-11-15, CC0 1.0 Universal.

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/headphones

Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/headphones_0.zip

Attribution is optional under CC0. This conversion separates the three mesh components, adds inferred earcup hinge pivots, sets width to 0.43 metres, centres the bounding box, resizes maps, packs ORM and converts DirectX normals to OpenGL.
