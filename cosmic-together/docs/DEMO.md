# Three-minute demo

1. Open `/demo`. The goal is a fall wedding under $200. Show the three recommendations and the evidence disclosure.
2. Select two cards to compare materials and formality. Change the budget to demonstrate hard filtering.
3. Select **Invite a friend**. Copy the link to another browser or device; enter a first name to join. Alternatively select **Add demo friend**, which labels Kass as simulated.
4. Vote for a choice. Use `@cosmo compare` in shared chat. The host selects **Refine with the circle’s votes**.
5. Open the chosen product and **Try it in your world**. Explore the 3D prototype. On a supported presentation device, select **Use my camera** and step back until shoulders and hips are visible.
6. Capture an image. Explain that it is private. Select **Share to circle**, review the snapshot, and explicitly confirm sharing.
7. Add the product to the bag, review the purchase, and **Confirm selection**. No payment or real order occurs.
8. Briefly visit `/ar/home/h01` for placement, rotation, finish, and dimensions; `/ar/gadgets/g01` for three captioned ghost-hand tutorials.

Before presenting, test camera permission, tracking, WebGL, snapshots, and second-device join on the actual hardware/network. Camera-free 3D and the local catalog are available without an external model API. The model and WASM are served with the app. Do not claim exact fit, measured room placement, live inventory, or live generative AI.

To reset a demo session, end its circle and clear the private shopping data in Preferences. `/demo` always starts a fresh recommendation pass but does not silently delete an existing cart or hosted circle.
